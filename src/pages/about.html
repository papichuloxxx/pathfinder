Our story

Pathfinder Driveways and Construction was established in 1998 as a fully Zimbabwean-owned company. Our expertise covers building construction, walling, tarmac surfacing, concrete work and brick paving.

The company is led by committed, knowledgeable directors and managers, and we hold every project to a high standard of quality.

We also manufacture our own pavers and bricks, and supply them either on their own or as part of a supply-and-fix installation.
