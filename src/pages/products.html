Frequently asked questions

Do you sell pavers only, or do you also supply and fix?

Both. We sell pavers and common bricks on their own, and we also offer a supply-and-fix service where our team installs them for you.

What types of pavers do you have?

Interlocking, 3D arrow, Holland, 3D diamond, hexagonal, bone & dog and star brick pavers. Ask us about colours and current stock.

What are the benefits of pavers?

Pavers look good, are easy to maintain and are cost-effective over the long term.

How long does it take to finish a driveway?

It depends on the area being covered, but a typical driveway takes about three working days.

Is supply and fix cheaper than buying pavers and installation separately?

Yes. When we supply and install, you get a discounted price compared with buying the two separately.
