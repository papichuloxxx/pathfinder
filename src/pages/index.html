About Pathfinder

A Zimbabwean paving and construction company

Pathfinder Driveways & Construction is a fully Zimbabwean-owned company, established in 1998. Our work covers brick paving, tarmac surfacing, building construction, walling and concrete work.

We manufacture our own pavers, so we can recommend the right product for your project and supply it directly.
