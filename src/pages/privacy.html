Privacy notice

A short explanation of what happens to the details you send us.

Who we are

Pathfinder Driveways & Construction (Private) Limited, No. 5 Partmac House, Eastlea Shops, Harare, Zimbabwe. Email admin@pathdriveway.co.zw or call 0242 788 113.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive your name and phone or WhatsApp number, plus your email address, project location and project description if you give them. If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive the details you share there.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and carry out any work you ask us to do. We do not sell your details or share them with anyone for marketing.

How it is sent and kept

The enquiry form emails your details to our business inbox. We keep them only for as long as we need them for your enquiry, quote or project records. To stop spam, the website briefly keeps a coded (hashed) record of the connection an enquiry came from, and deletes it within a day.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you by emailing admin@pathdriveway.co.zw.
